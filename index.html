About me

Curious since the Super Nintendo.

I’ve loved technology for as long as I can remember. One of my first consoles was a SNES, and I’ve been interested in how things work, and how they could work better, ever since.

That curiosity led me to design. I trained in fine arts in Italy and started out as a graphic and visual designer. Over time I moved into UX and product design, where solving real problems for real people became the most rewarding part of the job.

Today I’m a Product Designer at Sky in London. My visual design background helps me create experiences that people actually want to engage with. I believe good design makes everyday life a little better, and I try to bring passion, fairness and commitment to every team I’m part of.
